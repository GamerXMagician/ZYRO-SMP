Server Rules

📜 ZYRO SMP Rules

Read these before you apply. Following them keeps ZYRO fair and fun for everyone.

1
🤝 Respect Everyone

Treat all players and staff with respect. No bullying, harassment, hate speech, or toxicity.

2
🛠️ No Cheating or Hacks

No hacked clients, X-Ray, autoclickers, macros, or any unfair modifications.

3
🧨 No Exploiting

Don't abuse bugs, glitches, or duplication exploits. Report them to staff instead.

4
🏠 No Griefing

Don't destroy, alter, or steal from other players' builds without their permission.

5
💰 No Scamming

Honour your trades and deals. Tricking or deceiving players is not allowed.

6
🚫 No Spamming

Don't spam chat, commands, mentions, or emojis. Keep communication clean.

7
📢 No Advertising

Don't advertise other servers, Discord servers, or websites without permission.

8
⚔️ Fair PvP

Follow the server's PvP and event rules. No spawn-killing or unfair traps.

9
👑 Listen to Staff

Follow staff instructions — their decisions are final. Use tickets for reports and appeals.

10
🔥 Keep it Fun

Play fair, help newcomers, and keep ZYRO SMP a welcoming place for everyone.

⚠️ Breaking the rules may result in a warning, mute, kick, temporary ban, or permanent ban depending on the severity of the violation.
🔥 Have fun, play fair, and make ZYRO SMP an amazing community!
